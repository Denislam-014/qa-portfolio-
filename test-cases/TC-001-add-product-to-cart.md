TC-001: Add Product to Cart

Project: Educational Online Store
Module: Shopping Cart
Priority: High
Type: Functional Testing

Preconditions

* The online store is open.
* At least one product is available.

Test Steps

Step	Action	Expected Result
1	Open the product catalog	The product catalog is displayed
2	Select an available product	The product page opens
3	Click the Add to Cart button	The product is added to the cart
4	Open the shopping cart	The selected product is displayed
5	Check the product quantity	The quantity is correct

Expected Result

The selected product appears in the shopping cart with the correct quantity.

Actual Result: The product Sauce Labs Backpack was successfully added to the cart. The button changed to Remove, and the cart displayed 1 item.

Status

passed 

Note: This is an educational test case. It has not been executed against a real application.
